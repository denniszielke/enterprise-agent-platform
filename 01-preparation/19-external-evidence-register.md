# External Evidence Register

> Status: Draft
> Canonical owner: Product or program lead - name to be assigned
> Required reviewers: Owner of the requirement, decision, risk, estimate, gate, or claim supported
> Gate: Continuous
> Last reviewed: Not reviewed

The architecture harness may consume evidence created by research, reviews, experiments, implementation repositories, test systems, cloud environments, or operational processes. It does not generate implementation or operational evidence itself.

| ID | Evidence title | Class | External source | Requirement, realization, estimate, risk, or claim | Version and conditions | Result or finding | Limitation | Location | Reviewer | Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Evidence rules

- Keep implementation artifacts and sensitive evidence in their owning repository or approved evidence store.
- Record a stable reference, provenance, version, environment, data, method, result, reviewer, date, and limitation.
- A screenshot or generated diagram is not evidence of system behavior by itself.
- Evidence supports only its observed conditions and requires review when a linked input changes.
- Missing external evidence remains a gap; do not create success-shaped placeholders.
- Presentation claims link to this register and the canonical requirement or decision.
